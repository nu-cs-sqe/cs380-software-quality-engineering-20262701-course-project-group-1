This folder should include the system design. It can be in the form of design diagrams or a textual description of what classes the system shall have and their relationships.

It is optional for the team to document the design in this folder. A popular alternative is to document the design in the Project Management Board. 

### Gamble Cat (custom card)
When played, Gamble Cat flips a coin (50/50). Lucky: your turn ends immediately without drawing,
like a Skip. Unlucky: your turn only ends after you draw two cards in a row, and you cannot play
cards between the two draws. A defused Exploding Kitten counts as one of the two draws. Before
your first draw you may still play Attack, which ends your turn as usual and cancels the extra draw.
